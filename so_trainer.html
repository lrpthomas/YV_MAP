Welcome to the SO Trainer

A guided way to complete Ponderosa E.FACS service orders — and practice them.

  • Paste an SO request → it auto-routes to the right workflow.
  • The POINTER tells you which systems/fields to touch, in order; each step shows an example + where to find it.
  • The note builds live on the right — then Copy.
  • Corpus → Quiz me practices a real SO and scores you.
  • Ctrl/⌘+P jumps anywhere · ? shows all shortcuts · ⚑ flags anything wrong.
